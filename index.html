About the project

A local vision for more accessible care.

Geography should not be an insurmountable barrier to essential healthcare.

The proposed Sebei Community Hospital Project aims to develop a small not-for-profit health facility serving communities in the lower, rural belt of Sebei, Uganda. The concept responds to concerns about access to services for people who may face long or difficult journeys to reach care.

The project is in its early development stage. Land access has been identified by the project initiator, while land-title verification, legal registration, technical planning, construction, equipment and operational funding remain to be addressed.

Funding and partnership needs

Help move the project from concept toward implementation.

The project is seeking guidance and potential funding partners for feasibility, construction, essential medical equipment and future operational readiness. Grants, suitable investment and Islamic finance structures are of interest.

Indicative planning estimate

UGX 7.8–12.5 billion

Preliminary broad estimate for a small 20–30-bed hospital development, with a planning midpoint of approximately UGX 10.2 billion.

This is an early indicative estimate only, not a verified bill of quantities, quotation, approved budget or formal funding target. A feasibility study, design and detailed cost plan are still required.

Potential areas for support

  • Feasibility study, surveys and technical planning
  • Hospital construction and essential utilities
  • Medical equipment, furniture and supplies
  • Staffing, training and operational readiness planning
  • Governance, registration and regulatory preparation

Financing approaches of interest

Grant support, responsible investment, asset-based Islamic finance and partnership-based financing are being explored. No funding or financing has been confirmed.

The indicative range excludes land acquisition costs, major road works, a fully equipped operating theatre or specialist surgery, CT/MRI equipment and future expansion. Final costs may differ materially after professional assessment.
